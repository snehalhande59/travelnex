Frequently Asked Questions

How do I confirm my tour package or cab booking?
You can select your preferred tour package or service on the website, select your dates, enter passenger contact details and click Confirm. Your booking ID will be generated instantly and synced into your Customer Dashboard.
Are tolls, parking, and driver allowances included in outstation fares?
Yes! All TRAVELNEX fixed packages and outstation rates explicitly display all inclusions. Standard fuel, chauffeur allowance and state permits are covered transparently without hidden surprises.
What is the cancellation and refund policy?
We offer free cancellations up to 24 hours prior to local and outstation cab pickups, and up to 7 days before tour package start dates with full instant refunds back to your original payment method.
