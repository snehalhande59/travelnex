COMMON QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions regarding bookings, vehicles, corporate billing, and cancellations.

You can book online in less than 2 minutes via our website or Customer Dashboard. Simply select Local Taxi or Outstation Cabs, enter your pickup address, destination date and time, choose your vehicle tier (Sedan, Ertiga, Innova Crysta), and receive instant booking confirmation with driver assignment.
We offer 12, 17, 20 and 26-seater luxury Force Travellers and Urbanias for medium groups, as well as 32, 45, and 55-seater air-conditioned Volvo, Scania, and BharatBenz coaches for large tours, school excursions, and weddings. All buses include pushback seats, individual AC vents, and large luggage bays.
TRAVELNEX operates dedicated concierge assistance desks for Indian Railways (IRCTC) and Maharashtra State Transport (MSRTC). Our travel specialists guide you with route availability, Tatkal timing, Shivneri Volvo reservations, and group train booking assistance with full ticket confirmation updates.
Companies can sign up for the TRAVELNEX Corporate Portal to manage daily employee office commute rosters, executive airport concierge, and project site visits. Features include automated trip approvals, dedicated account managers, consolidated monthly invoicing, and full GST compliance.
You can cancel your booking directly from the Customer Dashboard under "My Bookings" with a single click. For cabs and outstation trips cancelled up to 6 hours before pickup, a 100% full refund is issued instantly to your original payment method with zero cancellation fees.
